Delete your LunaCycle data

LunaCycle (Android app by Manab Maity, package com.manabxd.periodtracker) stores your data on your phone, and, only if you enabled it, as an encrypted backup in your account. You can delete either without deleting your account.

Delete everything on this phone

  1. Open LunaCycle, menu icon (top left) › Settings › Data.
  2. Tap Delete all data and confirm.

All periods, daily logs and preferences on the phone are erased at once. If cloud backup is on, the encrypted cloud copy is erased too and you are signed out.

Delete only the cloud copy, keep the phone

  1. Menu › Backup & Sync.
  2. Tap Delete from the cloud (removes the backup and the account), or turn the Back up to my account switch off and tap Sign out to stop syncing while leaving the existing backup in place.

Delete single entries

Any period can be removed from the calendar (tap the day › Remove period day) or from Cycle history; any daily log can be cleared on the Log screen. Deletions sync to the backup if it is on.

By email

Email manab2001maity@gmail.com from your sign-in address and we delete your backup data within 30 days.

What is deleted, what is kept

  • Deleted: the data you chose: on-device records and preferences, and/or the encrypted backup documents in your account.
  • Kept: your account itself if you only deleted data. No copies are retained by us; backup data is encrypted with a passphrase only you hold, so nothing readable ever existed on our side.
