Delete your LunaCycle account

LunaCycle (Android app by Manab Maity, package com.manabxd.periodtracker). An account exists only if you turned on the optional cloud backup with Google sign-in. Everything else in the app works without an account.

Delete it in the app (takes seconds)

  1. Open LunaCycle and tap the menu icon (top left).
  2. Tap Backup & Sync.
  3. Scroll to Delete cloud backup & account and tap Delete from the cloud.
  4. Confirm. Your encrypted backup and your LunaCycle account are removed from Google's servers immediately.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, sign out and back in (Google requires a recent sign-in before an account can be deleted), then repeat step 3.

Or ask us by email

Email manab2001maity@gmail.com from the Google address you used to sign in, with the subject "LunaCycle account deletion". We delete the account and all associated backup data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted, what is kept

  • Deleted: your Firebase account (Google account identifier, name, email), all encrypted period and daily-log backup documents, deletion markers and the passphrase verifier. There is no retention period beyond the deletion itself; Google's backups of Firestore are purged within their standard 30-day window.
  • Kept: nothing on our side. The data on your phone stays until you delete it there (Settings › Data › Delete all data) or uninstall the app.
